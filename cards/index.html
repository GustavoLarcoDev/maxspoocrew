Why these are the size they are

Vistaprint wants 3.61 × 2.11 in uploaded for a 3.5 × 2 in card — the extra 0.055 in a side is bleed that gets cut away. Every mark on these six faces sits at least 0.18 in from the sheet edge, so nothing lands near the knife even if the cut drifts.

Classic and Quiet put no ink at the trim at all, which is the quiet advantage of a white card on white stock: a drifting cut has nothing to expose. Band is the exception — its red footer runs off three edges on purpose, and its bottom padding is set deliberately deeper than its top so the band still looks evenly set after the cut.

The QR on every back points at maxspoocrew.com/?utm_source=card, checked by decoding it back out of the finished PDF rather than trusting the source art.
