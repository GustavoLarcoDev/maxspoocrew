Where this stops, and what would extend it

This plans and measures. It does not publish, because publishing to a Facebook Page from your own tool needs a registered Meta app, business verification for Mike, and App Review for the posting permissions — plus a server to hold the tokens, which a static page cannot do. Say the word and I will build that side; the Meta paperwork is the part only you can do.

Your numbers live in this browser. They are saved locally, which means no account and no server, and also that they do not follow you to another machine. Use Export everything before you switch computers or clear your history.

Why not Hootsuite. Its cheapest plan is $99 a month per user, which is $1,188 a year — more than Jobber costs to run the whole business, for a job Meta Business Suite does free. Hootsuite earns its price when you run many accounts, a team and an approval flow. One truck is not that.
