Why it matters

It’s not just about the mess

Waste left in a yard is a health problem, a lawn problem, and a water problem — all at once.

Parasites don’t wait

Roundworm, hookworm, and giardia live in waste and can survive in soil for months, reinfecting your dog and reaching your kids.

It ruins your grass

Concentrated nitrogen burns yellow patches into turf and kills the roots underneath.

It runs off into our creeks

Rain carries bacteria straight into St. Louis area storm drains, streams, and eventually the Missouri and Mississippi.

You get your yard back

Barefoot in the grass, kids outside, a game of fetch that doesn’t end with a hose-down. That’s the whole point.

About us

The crew behind the scoop

Michael Harvey founded Max’s K9 Club and oversees Max’s Poo Crew. He’s a lifelong dog enthusiast with a deep appreciation for the remarkable abilities of working dogs — a passion that took hold watching search-and-rescue canines work in the days after 9/11.

His career kept him out of the K9 field at the time, but he never let the dream go. Successful real estate and software development businesses eventually gave him the room to build it for real.

Meet Max and Simon

Both dogs are nationally certified in human remains detection through NASAR, the National Association for Search and Rescue. They’re the namesakes and the mascots of every truck on the road.

Max’s K9 Club

Where Max’s Poo Crew Comes In

Max’s Poo Crew is the yard-care arm of Max’s K9 Club. It grew out of a simple observation: the families who trust us with training, boarding, and daycare also wanted their yards to be a safe, clean place for their dogs to be dogs. Same crew, same values — now keeping the grass clean across St. Louis and St. Charles County.

We believe that every dog is unique, and we customize our programs to suit their personality, abilities, and goals. With an emphasis on building trust and a strong bond between dog and handler, Max’s K9 Club is the ideal place to help your pup learn, grow, and thrive.
