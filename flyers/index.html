What to look for, in order

First, the headline — it is the only thing that gets read from three feet away, so if two flyers have interchangeable headlines one of them is wasted. Second, whether the piece commits: a flyer that argues price and health and trust argues nothing. Third, what the person holding it was doing thirty seconds earlier, which is what actually decides whether M beats H on a given noticeboard.

Before printing

Three things worth knowing

These are editable, and there are seven more flyers elsewhere

All ten are generated from HTML in print/src/ and share one stylesheet, print/src/_flyer.css — which is why ten different arguments still look like one company. Run python print/build.py and every PDF and preview on this page rebuilds. Flyers A through G, plus the two built on generated artwork, live over on the main print page.

Every price on these sheets is printed, not linked

Ten flyers now carry the rate table in ink. If the rates on the website change, they have to change in the sources too or the printed material starts lying — the affected files are H, I, K, L, O, P and Q. The QR code goes to maxspoocrew.com, so that part looks after itself.

Do not print all ten

Pick two or three that suit where you are actually leaving them, and print those in volume. Ten designs at a hundred copies each costs more per sheet than three at four hundred, and nine of them will sit in a box. The point of having ten is choosing, not distributing.
