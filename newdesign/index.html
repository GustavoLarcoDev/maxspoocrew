Ordering at Vistaprint

  1. Choose the glossy business card, standard 3.5 × 2 in, two-sided.
  2. Pick upload your complete design rather than a template.
  3. Upload the PDF — page 1 is the front, page 2 the back. The bleed is already in the file, so do not scale or nudge it on their canvas.

Each file is exactly 1083 × 633 px at 300 dpi (3.61 × 2.11 in), which is Vistaprint’s full-bleed size for this product. Artwork runs to all four edges and every line of copy sits at least 0.2 in in from them, so nothing lands near the cut.

The QR on every back points at maxspoocrew.com/?utm_source=card, the same tag the other business cards use, so a scan from any of them lands in one Analytics bucket.

Ordering a door hanger

  1. Most print shops sell “door hangers” as their own product at 3.75 × 8.25 in (or 4.25 × 11 in) with the knob hole already built into their die — you don’t cut it yourself.
  2. Upload the PDF as-is. The empty band at the top is intentional; say so if an artist at the print shop asks about it.
  3. If a shop instead wants you to mark the hole, it is a 1.375 in circle centered 1.75 in down from the top trim, slit straight up to the edge.

The QR on every hanger points at maxspoocrew.com/?utm_source=hanger, matching the tag the five original door hangers already use.
