HOW TO UPLOAD ON VISTAPRINT

  1. Standard Business Cards -> "Upload a full design"
  2. Front  = the FRONT pdf for that person
     Back   = the BACK pdf
  3. If it asks about bleed, the files already include it - do not let it
     scale or "fit to page". Choose 100% / actual size.
  4. Michael and Gustavo are separate orders (different front, same back).

BEFORE YOU ORDER

  the quote page. It is worth 10 seconds to confirm on your own device.

FILE SPECS

  Document size   3.61 x 2.11 in  (259.92 x 151.92 pt) - includes bleed
  Trim size       3.5 x 2 in - standard US business card
  Bleed           0.055 in on every edge
  Safe zone       0.125 in inside the trim - all text is inside it
  Colour          RGB (sRGB), Vistaprint converts to CMYK
  Fonts           embedded, nothing to install
  Logo            600+ dpi at printed size

                  (survives 25% damage), drawn as vector - not a picture,
