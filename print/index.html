Before printing or posting

Five things worth knowing

Eleven of these are now editable

Everything in Cards, rate sheet & direct mail, the two newest flyers, trifold L and the three vehicle magnets are generated from HTML in print/src/. Run python print/build.py and every PDF and preview rebuilds. Change a price in one file and it is correct in the artwork and on this page at the same time. The older pieces are still finished PDFs only — edits to those mean rebuilding them as sources first.

The website is live — this is now clear

The QR codes point at maxspoocrew.com, and the rebuilt site is deployed there: the redirects from the old /about, /pricing and /faq URLs are answering correctly. Scans and ad clicks land where they should. An earlier version of this page said the opposite — that was true when it was written and is not any more.

The free cleanup carries its condition everywhere now

The website offers a free first cleanup with any residential plan. Every piece here says it that way, including the two signs that used to promise it bare — on a board read in two seconds that was a promise you would have had to take back on the phone. A script checks all thirty-six pieces for it before they are published, so a shortened version cannot creep back in.

“No contracts” is gone from the print

Jobber has customers sign a service agreement at signup, so the claim was not true and it has been removed from all eleven trifolds and the signs. What replaces it is what is true: month to month, change frequency or pause with a week’s notice. The website still says it in nine places, including the Terms & Conditions page — that one is legal wording and worth reading before it is changed.

Each piece is tracked separately

The QR codes carry different utm_source tags, so Analytics will show whether a scan came from a trifold, a door hanger, a flyer, a sign or a menu. After one season that answers “which of these is worth reprinting” without guessing. All seven codes were decoded and verified, and every one is at least 20 mm printed, which is the floor for scanning at arm’s length.
