Before printing or posting

Three things worth knowing

Merge the website first

The QR codes point at maxspoocrew.com, which is correct — print lives for months and has to point at the real domain. But the new site is still sitting in the pull request, so right now a scan lands on the old site. Deploy it before the print run — and before the ads go live, because they send people to the same address and an ad click is money.

The free cleanup needs its condition

The website offers a free first cleanup with any residential plan. The ads and the service menu say it that way, and the trifolds carry it in the copy beside the offer. The two bandit signs still promise it bare — on a board read in two seconds that is a promise you would have to take back on the phone. Worth fixing before the signs are printed; say the word and it is a ten-minute change.

Each piece is tracked separately

The QR codes carry different utm_source tags, so Analytics will show whether a scan came from a trifold or a sign. After one season that answers “which of these is worth reprinting” without guessing. Both codes were decoded and verified.
