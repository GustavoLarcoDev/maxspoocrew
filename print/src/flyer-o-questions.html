Before you call

Eight questions, answered here.

These are the ones we get every week. If your answer is on this page, you do not need to phone us to find it out.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers are at work. We work the yard, latch the gate and text you when it is done.

What if my dog is outside?

Also fine. We board and train dogs for a living. Tell us if yours is nervous and we will work around it.

Where does it go?

With us. It is hauled off your property, not bagged into your own bin to wait for collection day.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. Pause for a holiday or cancel with a week’s notice, no conversation needed.

What if it has been months?

Start a plan and the first cleanup is free anyway, however bad. We have not been put off a yard yet.

Do you do one-time only?

Yes, $100 flat, no plan required and no sales call afterwards. Spring thaw is our busiest week for it.

What about winter?

We run all year. Deep snow can push a visit by a day, and we catch it up rather than skip it.

How do I pay?

Card or ACH at the start of each service month. One-time cleanups are due when the work is finished.
