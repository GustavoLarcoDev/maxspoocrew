Why it matters

It is not just an eyesore.

A yard that does not get cleared is a place your dog keeps reinfecting itself. That part does not fix itself with time.

Run by dog people, not a franchise

Parasites keep cycling

Roundworm and hookworm eggs pass out in waste and go back in through the paws and the mouth. Clearing the yard breaks a loop a dewormer alone does not.

Giardia lives in it

It survives in damp ground for weeks and moves easily between dogs sharing a yard. Multi-dog households pass it around the longest.

The grass burns out

High nitrogen and salts scorch the turf. The bare patch turns to mud, and the mud comes indoors.

It reaches the storm drain

Rain moves what is on the lawn into the gutter and then the creek. It is not fertiliser on the way — it is bacteria.
