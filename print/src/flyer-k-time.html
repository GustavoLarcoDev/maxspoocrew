The fifteen minutes is not really the point.

Almost nobody hires this out to save a quarter of an hour. They hire it out because it is the chore that never gets done on time — it gets done when it has become bad enough to notice, which is always later than it should be.

A schedule fixes that in a way willpower does not. The yard stops having a worst state, because nothing accumulates long enough to get there.
