Before you call

Six questions, answered.

The ones we get every week. If your answer is here, you do not need to phone us to find it out.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers are at work. We work the yard, latch the gate and text you when it is done.

What if my dog is outside?

Fine. We board and train dogs for a living. Tell us if yours is nervous and we will work around it.

Where does it go?

With us. Hauled off your property, not bagged into your own bin to wait for collection day.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. Pause or cancel with a week’s notice, no conversation needed.

What if it has been months?

Start a plan and the first cleanup is free anyway. We have not been put off a yard yet.

How do I pay?

Card or ACH at the start of each service month. One-time cleanups are due when the work is done.
